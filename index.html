game-of-life-np

Conway's Game of Life, twice, in Devanagari. One runs in the terminal on letterforms rasterized from a word you type. The other runs in the browser, where each live cell carries an अक्षर and colliding gliders assemble real Nepali verb forms.

नेपाली क्रिया रूपावली Verbs on a Life board

A conjugation reference wired to a B3/S23 board. Every component on the grid is an organism carrying a morpheme; when two collide their words are joined under Devanagari sandhi, and the result is checked against the full paradigm of the chosen root.

  • 24 verbs · 10 paradigms · 8 person and honorific grades
  • Forms derived from rules, not a hardcoded table
  • Includes a grammar reference and a drill mode

जीवन Life in the terminal

Type a word and its letters become the initial colony. Bowls and stems collapse quickly; the counters inside क and म tend to leave still lifes behind. Words dissolve differently depending on how they are shaped.

  • Arbitrary rulestrings, toroidal or dead edges
  • Three render styles, including a Devanagari age ramp
  • Detects still lifes, oscillators and extinction
